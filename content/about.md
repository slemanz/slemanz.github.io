## Who I am

I'm William Sleman, an Electrical Engineer who likes to understand things from the ground up. I work with embedded systems, hardware design and bare-metal firmware, because I want to know what every register does and why.

My approach is simple: read the datasheet, understand the peripheral, write the driver. No magic, no black boxes.

It's also how I learn. My personal projects started with a blinking LED and grew into a complete STM32 application with a UART bootloader written from scratch. The test I apply is always the same: if I can implement it, I understand it. If I can't, it's back to the reference manual.

## What I do

I'm a **Hardware Designer** at [Stara S.A.](https://www.stara.com.br/en), where I design ECUs and sensors for agricultural machinery. My boards end up in machines working in the field, where a firmware bug isn't a popup on a screen: it's a machine stopped in the middle of a harvest.

My day-to-day covers the full cycle: schematic capture, component selection, PCB layout in Altium, bare-metal firmware in C, and automated test systems for validation.

I started at Stara in 2022 as a Hardware Intern, soldering prototypes and writing firmware for basic peripherals. I became a Hardware Analyst, then a Hardware Designer, and today I take new sensor products from concept to working prototype.

## Who I am

I'm William Sleman, an Electrical Engineer. I ended up in embedded systems because it's a field where nobody gets to pretend they understand: either you know what every register does, or the hardware will prove that you don't.

That's why my way of working is to open the datasheet, understand the peripheral, and write the driver by hand. It takes longer than pulling in a library, but there are no black boxes left in the project, and when something breaks I know where to look.

That's how I learned, and how I keep learning. My personal projects started with a blinking LED and now include a complete STM32 application with a UART bootloader written from scratch. The test hasn't changed: if I can implement it, I understand it. If I can't, it means I still have something to learn.

## What I do

I'm an **Embedded Systems Engineer** at [Stara S.A.](https://www.stara.com.br/en), where I design ECUs and sensors for agricultural machinery. It's the kind of work that keeps you honest: my boards end up in machines out in the field, where a firmware bug isn't a popup on a screen, it's a harvest at a standstill.

I handle the whole cycle, from schematics and component selection to PCB layout in Altium and bare-metal firmware in C, plus the automated test systems that validate everything before it goes to the field.

I joined Stara in 2022 as an intern, soldering prototypes and writing firmware for simple peripherals. Since then I've moved through Hardware Analyst and Hardware Designer roles, and today I take new embedded products from concept to working prototype.
